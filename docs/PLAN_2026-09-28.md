## 0. What changed, and what did not

**The abstract stays exactly as written.** Its argument is correct and it is the argument.

What was wrong was the tense. Its closing promise —

> *"standardized multiparametric datasets could also provide a foundation for applying
> artificial intelligence methods capable of integrating heterogeneous results…"*

— is in the **future** tense. At an AI venue, a promise is not a contribution. The fix is not
to rewrite the abstract. The fix is to **deliver that paragraph** and present it in the past
tense, with an evaluation.

So the talk is the abstract's final paragraph, built and measured.

## 2. What a record is, and why that is the contribution

One row of `out/tables/d1_zeta_table.csv`, 39 columns. The parts that matter:

```
  measurement      quantity, value, value_text, unit, sd, n
  particle         material, functionalisation, supplier,
                   size_nominal_nm, size_measured_nm
  conditions       medium_text, medium_class, ionic_strength_mM,
                   pH, temperature_C, instrument
  ABSENCE          medium_reported, ionic_strength_reported, pH_reported,
                   temperature_reported, instrument_reported,
                   conditions_complete, ionic_strength_source
  evidence         evidence_quote (verbatim), evidence_location,
                   evidence_verified
  provenance       pmcid, doi, model, prompt_sha256, flags
```

### 2.1 Absence is a column, not a missing value

This is the part no literature-mined dataset ships and the part the abstract argues for.
A query for "PS at pH 7.4" must distinguish **pH 7.4 was reported** from **pH was never
reported**. Here it can: `pH_reported` is a boolean, `ionic_strength_source` records
`unreported` explicitly, and `value_text` carries `not printed` where no number exists.

Nothing is imputed. The repo has the receipt for why that rule exists: `normalise_unit`
returning `None` was once read as *"the paper printed nothing"* and filled with the
conventional default, storing a plausible number in the wrong unit under a flag asserting
nothing was printed. **Silent imputation is the default failure of literature-mined datasets.**

### 2.2 Every value is verbatim-checked

`evidence_verified` is **True for 1 890 of 1 890 rows** — **measured**. A row whose quote does
not appear in the source is dropped and counted, never silently kept.

### 2.3 The state of the corpus, measured today

| | |
|---|---|
| rows / papers / distinct materials | **1 890 / 491 / 151** |
| rows with a printed value | **1 384** (506 report a measurement with no retrievable number) |
| **fully conditioned rows** (`conditions_complete`) | **62 — 3.3 %** |
| ionic strength reported, row level | 269 — **14.2 %** |
| pH reported | 402 — 21.3 % |
| temperature reported | 529 — 28.0 % |
| medium reported | 1 572 — 83.2 % |
| instrument reported | 1 679 — 88.8 % |
| papers omitting ≥1 of the five conditions | **97.1 %** (`d1_stats.json`) |

**3.3 % is the abstract's thesis in one number.** Of 1 890 published surface measurements,
62 carry everything needed to interpret them.

### 2.4 Honest limits, stated on the dataset card

- The gold set used to score extraction was **annotated by Claude and not yet audited by a
  human**. Until the κ audit (§5 item 9) is filled, this caveat ships **with the dataset**.
- Only **2 distinct `prompt_sha256` values** appear across the table, so prompt provenance is
  currently coarse; `brief_sha256` per record is the finer key and not all records carry a
  current one. The card states the traced fraction and users can filter on it.
- 61 of 69 adjudication questions (`tests/edge/key.yaml`) are **unanswered**. Records touched
  by an open question carry the reader's best guess, flagged — never silently resolved.

## 3. Why chunk-and-embed cannot do this

This is the technical claim of the talk, and it is measurable on this corpus.

### 3.1 The measurement and its conditions are not in the same passage

**Measured today: 61.9 % of the 1 890 records (1 169) have evidence stitched from two or more
separate text pieces.** The value sits in Results or a table; the ionic strength sits in
Methods, sections away. **No chunk contains both.**

Worked example, row `PMC10458608-r1`, verbatim from the table:

> *"The zeta potential (ζ) of nanoparticles was also measured in water, CCM, and chyme. ||
> The zeta potential or surface charge of nanoparticles was measured from pH 2 to 7 in water,
> CCM, and chyme using a Stabino Zeta (Microtrac GmbH…)."*
> `evidence_location: Section 3.1 / Methods 2.1`

Retrieve either sentence alone and you get a fluent, on-topic passage about a zeta
measurement — with **no number**, **no ionic strength**, and a pH given as a range. The record
says so explicitly: `value_text = "not printed"`, `ionic_strength_source = "unreported"`.

### 3.2 Absence has no passage to retrieve

A question whose true answer is *"never reported"* has no chunk that says so. Naive RAG
retrieves the nearest number and the reader states it. The KB answers from a boolean.

This is directly measurable as a **fabrication rate with ground truth** — §4, query kind B.

### 3.3 Numeric comparison is not a similarity operation

*"Which papers report PS zeta at ionic strength between 100 and 200 mM?"* is a range filter
over a parsed quantity. Embeddings cannot do it, and this is precisely the comparability the
abstract asks for.

## 4. The evaluation — the part that makes it a talk

Two systems, one query set, ground truth from the gold 50 and from the dataset's own
`*_reported` booleans.

| kind | query | n, *projected* | ground truth |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — conditioned lookup** | "zeta of PS-COOH in seawater, and at what ionic strength?" | ~30 | `gold_50.csv` |
| **B — absence** | "what ionic strength was used in PMC…?" where none was reported | ~30 | `ionic_strength_reported = False` |
| **C — comparison** | "papers reporting PS zeta at I between 100–200 mM" | ~10 | structured filter over the table |

Metrics: answer accuracy, **fabrication rate on kind B**, and citation correctness — does the
answer trace to a quote that actually exists in the source?

### 4.1 The baseline must be strong, or the result is worthless

I own both systems and one of them is mine. The obvious Q&A attack is *"you strawmanned the
baseline."* So the baseline is built to win where it can:

- section-aware chunking with JATS metadata retained (not blind 512-token splits)
- a competent embedding model, **and** a reranking pass
- the same reader model answering from retrieved passages
- tuning procedure written down and reported

And **report where RAG wins.** It will: qualitative and contextual questions the KB has no
field for — mechanisms, why an author chose a medium, what a discussion argues. Saying so is
what makes the kind-A/B result credible.

`torch>=2.2` is already a dependency (sbi pulls it), so a local embedding model adds no heavy
dependency and preserves the property that **no API key is configured**.

### 4.2 Contrast case, if time allows

`ARCHITECTURE_PLAN.md` §8 already names it: **caNanoLab** and **eNanoMapper/NanoCommons** hold
nanomaterial characterisation records *with* conditions. They are the existing places where
the field did this right, and the honest framing is "here is what exists, here is what it does
not cover." Nice-to-have, not a gate.

## 9. What would change my mind

- If the naive RAG baseline matches the KB on kind A, the extraction layer is not earning its
  cost for lookup and the contribution narrows to kinds B and C. Say so.
- If the κ audit lands κ < 0.6, the gold set is not ground truth, every extraction score is
  provisional, and the dataset card says it before anyone else finds out.
- If kind-B fabrication is rare because the baseline reader abstains well, then absence is
  cheaper to handle than claimed and the argument rests on kind C instead.
