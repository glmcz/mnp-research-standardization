# Case 11 — environmental-matrix


**mode:** effluent before/after treatment


## 1. Why this case

Ten rows span five industrial effluents -- resin, paint, pharmaceutical, PVC and textile -- each sampled before and after in-house wastewater treatment, so the zeta potential belongs to whatever particles the effluent carried, in a matrix whose ionic strength and pH are never given. Comparing it with a lab-prepared dispersion elsewhere in the corpus would compare two kinds of experiment even where the numbers look alike. The case is recognised by what the paper prints ("effluent"), not by medium_class: that field is a reader's label, and the re-read filed these rows under `other` where the earlier reading had environmental_water.
