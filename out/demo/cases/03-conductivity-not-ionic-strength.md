# Case 03 — conductivity-not-ionic-strength


**mode:** a number printed, not the one needed


## 1. Why this case

Two aqueous media here are named by their conductivity rather than by a stated ionic strength: tap water at 230 uS/cm, a NaCl solution at 4,000 uS/cm. Conductivity and ionic strength are related, but only through an assumed ionic composition the paper never gives, so none of these rows converts to a usable ionic strength here. This is a paper printing a number, and that number still not being the one the standardisation walk needs.

- doi: 10.1038/s41598-025-30476-6
- year: 2025
- journal: Scientific Reports
