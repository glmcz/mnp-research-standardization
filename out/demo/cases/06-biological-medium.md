# Case 06 — biological-medium


**mode:** corona: different particles converge


## 1. Why this case

Seventy-two of this paper's ninety rows disperse particles in a culture medium, the setting where a protein corona forms around whatever material or functionalisation the particle started with. That is the mechanism the abstract calls convergence: chemically distinct particles arrive at a similar surface charge once serum proteins coat them, so the zeta value alone stops distinguishing the particle that produced it. None of these rows states an ionic strength either, for the same reason as the buffer case: a culture medium is a named liquid whose composition is not printed.
