# Case 01 — fully-conditioned


**mode:** the rare success


## 1. Why this case

Two PEGylated carboxylate polystyrene particles, nominally 20 and 40 nm, give -2.91 and -2.04 mV in the same 15x diluted PBS (~10 mM NaCl, pH 7.4) at 25 C on the same Zetasizer NanoZS: every condition is printed, and these two rows are one of only two fully comparable pairs in the 1,908-row corpus (`comparability.comparable_census`). Their measured sizes, 56.2 and 60.9 nm, put kappa*a at 9.2 and 10.0, so Henry's f differs by about 1% and the key can place both values on one scale. The paper's 100 nm sibling in the same medium (139.8 nm, kappa*a 23) is refused on exactly that axis: a fully conditioned row is necessary for a comparison, and still not enough once the particles sit in different conversion regimes.
