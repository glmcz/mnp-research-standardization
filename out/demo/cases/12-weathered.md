# Case 12 — weathered


**mode:** aging changes charge and has no field


## 1. Why this case

Twenty-eight of this paper's forty-two rows carry "aged", "UV" or "weather" somewhere in their functionalisation or particle name, because ageing changes surface charge and this dataset has no field to record it -- not a duration, not a UV dose, nothing. A row that says "UV-aged PS" is not the same particle as "PS", and the standardisation walk has no condition that can tell the two apart the way it tells media or pH apart.
