## 3. The numbers that matter

All measured on 858 XMLs / 491 papers / 1 890 rows. Nothing estimated.

| claim | figure |
|---|---|
| comparable row pairs, of 1 785 105 | **16** |
| of those, zeta-vs-zeta | **6** |
| of those six, crossing a paper boundary | **0** |
| papers reporting a zeta without naming the conversion model | **93,2 %** (452 of 485) |
| fully conditioned, **per paper** | **2,3 %** |
| fully conditioned, per row | 3,3 % |
| intraclass correlation of reporting | **0,78 – 0,94** |
| effective sample size | **517 – 588**, not 1 890 |

**The headline is the comparability one.** No zeta potential in this corpus can be compared
with a zeta potential from a different paper. Not few — zero. And it survives attack: the
median pair is blocked by seven rules independently, and dropping every rule about identity,
agreement, measurand and physics together moves 16 → 51. Only **letting an unreported
condition acquire a default** moves it 5 100×, to 81 624. The verdict is driven by the
literature's silence, not by strictness.

## 4. What was decided, and must not be re-litigated

- **A condition's scope is the sentence the worker quoted**, not the paragraph the row cites
  (Martin, 2026-09-18). Both gates escalated the wider version and both were right.
- **The converter does not earn the reading path.** E6 measured it: the 12 000-token budget
  binds on 12–13 papers of 491, so a perfect selector is worth ~1,6 % of end-to-end tokens.
- **The graph is not a selector.** It ties with plain BM25 once query leakage is closed; its
  apparent edge was the leaked value. Use BM25 in the retrieval cells.
- **The graph *is* a representation of relations**, which is a different and better claim.
  Whether two rows may be compared lives in the join, not in any passage, so retrieval cannot
  reach it — the same structural argument as absence.
- **Cell A (long context) is cut.** E1b's arithmetic already says the corpus is 2,64× a 1 M
  window; running it costs ~2,4 M tokens to re-establish division.
- **Per-paper figures are primary**, with cluster-bootstrap intervals and the design effect
  stated. Anything computed as `√(p(1−p)/1890)` in this repo is wrong.

## 5. The recurring defect, three instances in one session

**A field that summarises is not the thing the decision was made from.**

1. Card 2 named one caller of `verify_row` from memory; there are three, and the unnamed one
   was the measurement its own failure condition depended on.
2. The engineer gate called four rows a worker's invention; the paper prints the number.
3. **I did the same**, classifying 14 rows as imputed from nothing by reading only the
   `medium_text` column. Both examples were legitimate — one paper prints the full PBS
   composition that derives to exactly the stored value.

**Rule for the next session:** list callers from a grep run *while writing*, and read the paper
before calling a provenance claim false. The wrong conclusion is recorded in `FINDINGS.md`
E2d rather than deleted, deliberately.

Second recurring rule: **run it twice, two ways.** A duplicate cluster-bootstrap implementation
caught a real bug — resampling papers then calling `groupby` on the cluster key silently
cancels the replacement and narrows the interval.
