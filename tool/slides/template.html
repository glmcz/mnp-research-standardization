09Act II · reading what isn’t there

Absence has a size.

Without ionic strength, one printed zeta becomes an interval of ±{{h.hw_f}}. Without the conversion model as well, as in {{h.z4}} rows, ±{{h.hw_z4}}.

comparability.debye_length_nm, henry_f · harmonise case Z4 · out/review/harmonised_all_rows.csv · REVIEW.md §5.4, §5.5

Speaker notes

This is why ionic strength costs the most. Henry’s function converts the measured mobility into zeta; it runs from {{h.f_lo}} to {{h.f_hi}} depending on the particle’s size against the Debye length, and the Debye length depends on ionic strength. Without ionic strength, f can sit anywhere in the band, which is ±{{h.hw_f}} on the printed value. When the conversion model is unknown too, as in {{h.z4}} rows, it is ±{{h.hw_z4}}.

docs/DECISIONS.md EX-12, EX-13, EX-14

Speaker notes

This was the most uncomfortable finding. The verifier flags any value it cannot find in the text, and the workers were told to act on flags. When a guard failed to recognise a correct value, written in molar, in kelvin or with an exponent, a worker deleted the value to clear the flag, and the data moved toward absence, the very quantity we measure. Then our own fix widened one guard too far and let a co-exposure concentration through as an ionic strength.

Speaker notes

Same model, same questions, two ways of handing it the evidence. In cell D the reader gets the table, and for comparability the code’s verdict is in the prompt, so its perfect score is transcription, not judgement. Cell B retrieves lines from the papers and never finds a comparable pair; its raw accuracy is high only because it calls everything not comparable. Comparability lives in the join of two rows, and no passage of text contains it.

Can, and can’t.

Agents made the reading possible at scale. Code made it checkable.

  • Measure the gap.A missing condition is recorded as a value, row by row.
  • Rank what is missing.And find the pairs one reported number would decide.
  • Rebuild everything deterministically.report reproduce, byte for byte.

Can’t

  • Fill in a missing condition.A default is a guess, and the dataset refuses it.
  • Replace a human check.The audit is still to come.
  • Decide comparability by itself.Code does that; the model reads the verdict.

Speaker notes

Agents made it possible to read the corpus at this scale with every value traceable to its line. They cannot supply a condition a paper did not report, and they should not decide comparability on their own. The verdicts come from code that anyone can rerun, and the one check that needs a person is still ahead of us.

16Act III · what AI can and can’t do

The call: a minimal reporting standard.

Report the five conditions, identify the particle, and name the conversion model.

The five conditions

  • the medium
  • ionic strength as a number, not a buffer name
  • pH
  • the temperature of the measurement itself
  • the instrument

The particle

Supplier, lot and surface chemistry. Case 14’s two lots of {{c14.size_nominal}} nm polystyrene pass all five conditions and still differ by {{c14.gap}} mV.

The conversion model

Smoluchowski, Hückel or Henry’s function. Without it and without κa, as in {{h.z4}} rows, a printed zeta is only known to ±{{h.hw_z4}}.
