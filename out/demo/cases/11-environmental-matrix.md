# Case 11 — environmental-matrix


**mode:** effluent before/after treatment


## 1. Why this case

Ten rows span five industrial effluents -- resin, paint, pharmaceutical, PVC and textile -- each measured before and after in-house wastewater treatment. This is a zeta potential measured on whatever microplastics were actually present in the water, not on a particle dispersed on purpose in the lab, so its medium_class is environmental_water rather than a prepared dispersion. Comparing it with a lab-prepared row elsewhere in the corpus would compare two different kinds of experiment even where the numbers happen to look similar.
